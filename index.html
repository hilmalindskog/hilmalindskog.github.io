Hilma C. Lindskog

Doctoral Student  ·  Department of Political Science, University of Gothenburg

I am a PhD candidate at the Department of Political Science at the University of Gothenburg since 2022. My dissertation focuses on the influence of education on political behavior, situated at the intersection of political sociology and political economy. I investigate this topic through social processes occurring both during the time spent in education and in the transition to the labor market. I work primarily with quantitative methods, including large-scale administrative data.

I supervise theses at bachelor and master level. Students interested in supervision are welcome to reach out.
